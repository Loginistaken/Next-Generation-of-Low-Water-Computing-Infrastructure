# 2. PURPOSE OF THE ARCHITECTURE

The purpose of the EL-40 + PBUA concept is to explore whether future computing infrastructure could reduce unnecessary energy consumption and therefore reduce the amount of heat that must subsequently be removed.

The architecture proposes a two-stage strategy:

### Stage 1 — Reduce Heat Creation

The proposed computing architecture seeks to reduce unnecessary energy expenditure through:

- EL-40 logic;
- PBUA information architecture;
- photonic information transport;
- efficient interconnects;
- reduced data movement;
- reduced processing overhead;
- architecture-aware scheduling;
- high-speed optical communication.

### Stage 2 — Remove Remaining Heat Efficiently

The proposed infrastructure then explores:

- direct-to-chip cooling;
- dielectric-fluid cooling;
- closed-loop refrigeration;
- dry cooling;
- geothermal heat rejection;
- heat recovery and reuse.

The overall objective is to investigate whether future AI infrastructure could simultaneously improve computational efficiency and reduce dependence on evaporative cooling.

# 14. DRY-COOLING DISCLAIMER

The proposed dry-cooling architecture rejects heat into outside air rather than relying on evaporative cooling towers.

Dry cooling may substantially reduce water consumption under appropriate conditions.

However, its performance depends strongly on:

- outdoor temperature;
- humidity;
- heat-exchanger surface area;
- airflow;
- fan power;
- thermal load;
- seasonal conditions;
- equipment sizing.

Very high ambient temperatures can make air-based heat rejection more difficult.

Therefore, "near-zero water" remains a **design objective**, not a guaranteed result.
